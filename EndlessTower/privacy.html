Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

This policy explains how the game Endless Tower (package com.cnp.stack, developer CNP) handles information. Contact: love.kor.1976@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We (the developer) do not collect personal information. The game has no account sign-up and we run no server.

2. Information kept on your device

Your top 10 records (blocks and area), the towers of those records shown in the background, and your settings (music and sound-effect volume) are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

3. Google Play Games (optional)

Signing in to Google Play Games happens only if you tap SIGN IN on the GLOBAL tab of the ranking. If you sign in, Google stores your Play Games profile (display name, avatar, player ID) and the scores the game submits (number of blocks), and your score can appear on the public leaderboard. You can change your Play Games visibility in the Play Games settings. The whole game can be played without signing in. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • A banner ad is shown at the top of the screen.
  • Every ad request is marked as directed to children and as under the age of consent, and only ads rated G are requested. Personalized (interest-based) ads are not shown.
  • The game does not use the advertising ID (the permission is removed).
  • To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may process device information other than the advertising ID, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostics. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Permissions

The game does not ask for storage, location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions, and does not use the advertising ID permission. Internet and network-state access are used by the ads and Play Games SDKs.

6. Sharing and international transfer

Only the information in sections 3 and 4 is processed by Google LLC (USA) as described there. We do not share information with anyone else.

7. Children

The game is for everyone, including children. It follows Google Play's Families policy, and we aim to comply with COPPA and the GDPR. We do not collect personal information from anyone, ads use child-protection settings, and Play Games sign-in is optional (parents and guardians can keep it off).

8. Contact

CNP — love.kor.1976@gmail.com

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
