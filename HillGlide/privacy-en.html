Hill Glide Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This policy explains how CNP ("the developer") handles information in the Android game "Hill Glide" (com.cnp.hillglide, "the app").

1. Information we collect

The developer does not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up, and the developer runs no servers.

2. Information kept on your device only

Your best scores, quest progress, unlocked looks and settings (music, sound effects, language) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

3. Google Play Games Services (leaderboard, optional)

If you sign in to Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (gamer name, profile picture, player ID) and your scores are stored by Google for the leaderboard and may be shown to other players. You can change your profile visibility in Play Store → profile picture → Settings → General → Play Games profile. Every part of the game works without signing in.

4. Ads (Google AdMob, optional)

The app shows an ad only when you choose to tap [Support]; not watching ads never limits the game. To show the ad, Google AdMob (Google LLC) may collect the following and send it to Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • IP address and an approximate location based on it
  • Ad interactions in the app, such as views and taps
  • Diagnostics and crash logs

This information is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and how Google uses information from partner apps (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can turn off personalised ads by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device's Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.

5. Permissions

The app does not ask for storage, location, camera or contacts permissions. Internet, network state and advertising ID permissions are used by the ads and Play Games SDKs.

6. Sharing and international transfer

The information in sections 3 and 4 is sent to and processed by Google LLC (United States). Nothing else is shared with third parties.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
